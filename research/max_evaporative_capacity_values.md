## Notes on Values

- Values are estimates based on published literature on evaporative cooling mechanisms
- Species with "none" for sweating capacity and no panting strategy have minimal evaporative capacity (~1.0x BMR)
- Birds with gular flutter typically achieve 2-3x BMR evaporative cooling
- Large desert mammals with sweating can achieve 2.5-3x BMR
- Semi-aquatic species rely on conductive cooling through water immersion, limiting evaporative scope
- Cold-adapted species (Pallas's cat, Rock ptarmigan) have minimal evaporative cooling capacity
